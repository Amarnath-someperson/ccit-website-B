Real-world examples of how parallel execution reduces development time from hours to minutes.

One of the most powerful patterns in agentic development is the use of parallel subagents — multiple AI agents working simultaneously on different parts of a problem.

## The Pattern

Instead of a single agent working sequentially through tasks, a coordinator agent breaks the work into independent chunks and dispatches them to specialized subagents.

## Real Results

In our benchmarks, parallel subagent execution reduced feature implementation time by 70% compared to sequential approaches, while maintaining or improving code quality through diverse solution strategies.
