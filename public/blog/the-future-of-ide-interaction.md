title: The Future of IDE Interaction

Exploring how natural language interfaces are reshaping developer tools.

The traditional IDE — with its menus, toolbars, and keyboard shortcuts — is giving way to a new paradigm: conversational development environments where you describe what you want in natural language and the system builds it.

## Natural Language as the New API

Just as REST APIs decoupled frontends from backends, natural language interfaces are decoupling intent from implementation. Developers can now express goals in plain English, and AI agents translate those goals into working code.

## The Blended Approach

The most effective environments blend traditional controls with AI assistance — giving developers the freedom to switch between direct manipulation and natural language based on what's most efficient for the task at hand.

We believe the future IDE won't be a single interface, but a fluid environment that adapts to how you work best.
