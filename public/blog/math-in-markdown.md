How to write beautiful mathematical expressions in your blog posts using KaTeX syntax.

## Inline Math

You can write inline math like $E = mc^2$ or $\int_0^1 x^2 dx$ directly in your sentences.

## Display Math

For larger equations, use display mode:

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

## Matrices

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Fractions and Roots

Inline fractions look great too: $\frac{a}{b}$ and $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$.

Display fractions:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left( \int_{a}^{x} f(t) dt \right) = f(x)
$$

## Greek Letters

Common symbols work naturally: $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \theta, \lambda, \pi, \sigma, \omega$.

Upper case too: $\Gamma, \Delta, \Theta, \Lambda, \Pi, \Sigma, \Omega$.
